# Agora - Gathering Place for Ai Geeks

Agora is the premier marketplace, sandbox, and deployment platform for artificial intelligence enthusiasts, creators, and engineers.

## Features
- **Store & Discovery**: Browse cutting-edge models across reasoning, coding, video, vision, audio, and science.
- **⚡ Discounts & Deals**: Dedicated section for exclusive AI model discounts, bundle savings, and flash promotions.
- **Interactive Sandbox**: Test models directly with local & cloud inference simulations.
- **Model Launcher & Telemetry**: Monitor local GPU, VRAM, and tokens/sec telemetry during inference.
- **Deployment Engine**: Deploy models as serverless APIs with custom auth keys and endpoint monitoring.
- **Community & Workshop**: Share LoRAs, fine-tuning recipes, and prompts.
